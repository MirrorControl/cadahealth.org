THE CRISIS THAT CREATED CADA

The documentation gap is federally documented and financially catastrophic.

The HHS Office of Inspector General audited ABA Medicaid billing across four states. What they found was not fraud. It was documentation — by people who were never trained on what documentation actually means in a billing context.

OIG AUDIT FINDINGS — WHAT THE FEDERAL GOVERNMENT FOUND

Across Indiana, Wisconsin, Maine, and Colorado, every sampled enrollee-month had at least one improper or potentially improper ABA payment. The combined financial exposure exceeded $500 million. Indiana is actively recouping money from individual providers. Colorado added pre-payment review in July 2025.

The most common finding: session notes that did not meet payer documentation requirements. The staff writing those notes were not negligent. They were untrained. That is the gap CADA fills.

THE CURRICULUM

6 modules. Every role. Every audit risk. Every code change.

Text-based, self-paced, scenario-rich. Each module follows a proven 7-part architecture: consequence hook, learning objectives, core content with embedded knowledge checks, worked example, module scenario, job aid, and graded quiz.

MODULE 1
The ABA Documentation Standard
What session notes actually are in a billing context, the 9 most common RBT documentation errors, and the documentation chain that links every note to every dollar billed.
Job aid: CADA Session Note Audit Checklist
MODULE 2
Prior Authorization: The Approval Lifecycle
How to read an authorization, track units against delivered services, recognize expiration risk before it becomes a recoupment demand, and submit initial vs renewal requests.
Job aid: CADA Authorization Tracking Matrix
MODULE 3
ABA Billing Fundamentals for Non-Clinical Staff
CPT codes 97151 through 97158 in plain language, why a one-unit mismatch generates a denial, how session notes map to billed codes, and concurrent billing restrictions.
Job aid: CADA CPT Code Reference Card
MODULE 4
Medicaid Compliance and Audit Defense
What OIG audits actually look like from inside a clinic, the difference between pre-payment review and retrospective audit, and what to do in the first 72 hours when a records request arrives.
Job aid: CADA Pre-Submission Audit Checklist
MODULE 5 — LAUNCH HOOK
The 2027 CPT Code Transition
January 1, 2027: 6 new adaptive behavior codes, 2 deleted, 97155 split in two. Every authorization written under old codes that extends into 2027 references codes that no longer exist. This module covers what to do before December 31.
Job aid: CADA 2027 CPT Transition Checklist
MODULE 6 — CAPSTONE
Payer Rules, State Variations, and Real-World Scenarios
Why Medi-Cal requires fields United Healthcare does not, how to build a payer rule binder, the documentation coordinator's role in a first-level appeal, and a multi-payer capstone scenario integrating all six modules.
Job aid: CADA Payer Rules Reference Guide
THE ASSESSMENT SYSTEM

Three layers of practice. The credential that actually prepares you.

The most common complaint across comparable certifications: learners cannot see which answers they got wrong. CADA gives rationales on every wrong answer, at every layer, from the first knowledge check through the final assessment.

Module Quizzes — 30 Total
5 graded questions per module, embedded throughout the curriculum. Rationales on every wrong answer. Scores and weak areas tracked throughout.
Full Practice Exam — 12 Questions
Available after all 6 modules are complete. Generates a Weak Areas summary pointing back to the specific module numbers where you need to review.
Final Assessment — 20 Questions
80% pass threshold (12 of 15). No rationales during the exam — this is the real test. Weak areas summary available immediately after. 72-hour retake wait on a failed attempt.
Ungraded Knowledge Checks
Two knowledge checks per content section — 10 per module, 60 total — with immediate rationale feedback. Sections gate until checks are answered, ensuring comprehension before advancing.
Job Aid With Every Module
A printable, desk-ready reference with every module: checklist, comparison table, decision tree, or reference guide. These are what documentation coordinators keep and share long after the credential is earned.
Digital Certificate and LinkedIn Badge
PDF certificate with your name, credential ID, issue date, expiration date, and CareGuard seal. Shareable digital badge for LinkedIn. Credential ID verifiable at cadahealth.org/verify.
